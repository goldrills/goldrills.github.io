Jack Bowser

Jack was born in Akron, Ohio and raised in Cary, North Carolina. He is currently a fifth-year student in the Bachelor of Architecture program at Pratt Institute in Brooklyn, New York, graduating in May 2027. He has studied abroad in Mexico City, Oaxaca de Juárez, and Rome.

Within architecture, Jack is interested in pursuing historic preservation, combining his interests in architecture and history. His favorite architect is Carlo Scarpa, and his favorite project is Scarpa's Castelvecchio Museum in Verona, Italy.

He works comfortably in Rhino, Revit, the Adobe suite, and Microsoft Office. He is additionally comfortable with AI tools such as Claude Cowork/Code and ComfyUI, when appropriate.

Outside of studio, Jack is a devoted Cleveland Browns fan who usually spends his Sundays cheering them on from his desk. He loves to create and build, always chasing a new project to keep his creative output flowing. He also loves to read and learn across both fiction and history. Beyond the occasional Wikipedia rabbit hole, his favorite books are Snow by Orhan Pamuk and There's Always This Year by Hanif Abdurraqib.
